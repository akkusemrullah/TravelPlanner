# Google Play / App Store – English store listing

**App name (30):** Travel Planner – Trip Plans

**Short description (80):** Trip plans, budget, documents and map in one place – no account, no ads.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Plan your trip in one place. Travel Planner keeps your day-by-day plan, budget, documents and places to visit in your pocket. Everything stays on your device: no account, no ads, no tracking.

✈️ DAY-BY-DAY PLAN
• Timed activities for every day, reorder with drag & drop
• Link activities to saved places and see each day's route on the map
• A "Today" card while you travel

🌤️ WEATHER
• A forecast for every trip day (within 16 days) and past-years averages further out

💰 BUDGET & EXPENSES
• A currency per trip, expenses in any currency with automatic conversion
• Spending by category and per day
• Alerts at 80% and 100% of the budget
• Split costs with friends: who paid, who owes whom

🎒 SMART PACKING LIST
• Suggestions based on weather and trip length (umbrella, coat, plug adapter…)
• Save your own packing templates

📎 DOCUMENTS
• Tickets, bookings, visa PDFs and photos – available offline

🗺️ MAP
• Visited and planned places in different colours
• Long-press the map to add a place

📤 SHARE & BACK UP
• Share your plan as text or PDF
• Back up everything to one file and restore it on another device

🌙 Dark theme · Turkish and English · Screen-reader support
