# Google Play / App Store – English store listing

**App name (30):** Travel Planner – Trip Plans

**Short description (80):** Trip plans, budget, documents and AI suggestions in one place – ad-free.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Plan your trip in one place. Travel Planner keeps your day-by-day plan, budget, documents and places to visit in your pocket. Use it without an account and your data stays on your device – or create a free account to sync across your devices and plan together with friends. No ads, no tracking.

✈️ DAY-BY-DAY PLAN
• Timed activities for every day, reorder with drag & drop
• Link activities to saved places and see each day's route on the map
• A "Today" card and morning reminders while you travel

🤖 PLAN WITH AI
• Answer 4 questions and get a day-by-day plan, places to visit, meal stops and things to do before you leave
• Suggested places are checked on the map; pick what you like and add it to your trip
• 5 plans a day with an account

👥 SYNC & SHARE
• The same trips on all your devices; offline changes are sent once you're back online
• Invite friends with a code as editors or viewers

🌤️ WEATHER
• A forecast for every trip day (within 16 days) and past-years averages further out

💰 BUDGET & EXPENSES
• A currency per trip, expenses in any currency with automatic conversion
• Spending by category and per day
• Alerts at 80% and 100% of the budget
• Split costs with friends: who paid, who owes whom
• Export expenses as CSV

🎒 SMART PACKING LIST
• Suggestions based on weather and trip length (umbrella, coat, plug adapter…)
• Save your own packing templates

📎 DOCUMENTS
• Tickets, bookings, visa PDFs and photos – available offline

🗺️ MAP
• Visited and planned places in different colours
• Long-press the map to add a place

📤 SHARE, BACK UP, UNDO
• Share your plan as text or PDF
• Back up everything to one file and restore it on another device
• Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days – restore them or delete them permanently

🔒 PRIVACY
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking; data on servers in the EU (Frankfurt)
• Delete your account and data from within the app
• In-app help centre, privacy policy, KVKK notice and terms of use

🌙 Dark theme · Turkish and English · Screen-reader support
