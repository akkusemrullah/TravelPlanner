### 2. İşlenen kişisel veriler
Uygulamayı hesap açmadan kullandığında verilerin yalnızca cihazında kalır ve tarafımızca işlenmez. Hesap açtığında aşağıdaki veriler işlenir:
- Kimlik: ad soyad.
- İletişim: e-posta adresi.
- Kullanıcı işlem verileri: seyahat bilgileri (ad, ülke, şehir, tarihler, bütçe, açıklama), gün gün plan, kayıtlı yerler ve konumları, harcama kayıtları (tutar, para birimi, kategori, açıklama, ödeyen), katılımcı adları, görevler, bavul şablonları, yapay zekâ planlayıcısına verdiğin cevaplar ve notlar.
- Görsel ve belge verileri: seyahate eklediğin fotoğraflar ve belgeler (bilet, rezervasyon, vize vb.), Google ile giriş yaptıysan profil fotoğrafının adresi.
- İşlem güvenliği: hesap kimliği, oturum bilgileri, IP adresi ve sunucu kayıtları, yapay zekâ kotası için cihaz kimliğinden türetilen geri çevrilemeyen özet ve günlük kullanım sayısı.

Uygulama özel nitelikli kişisel veri talep etmez. Eklediğin belgelerde (ör. pasaport, vize) bu tür veriler bulunabilir; bu belgeler yalnızca senin talimatınla saklanır ve başka bir amaçla işlenmez. Gerekmedikçe bu tür belgeleri eklememeni öneririz.

Kişisel verilerin hesabın açık olduğu sürece saklanır. Silinen kayıtlar en geç 30 gün içinde kalıcı olarak silinir. Hesabını sildiğinde sahibi olduğun (başka bir üyeye devretmediğin) seyahatler, belgeler ve profil bilgilerin derhâl silinir; belge ve fotoğraf dosyaları buluttan derhâl, teknik bir aksaklık olursa en geç 2 gün içinde silinir. Silinen belgelerin buluttaki dosyaları en geç 31 gün içinde silinir. Kanunen saklanması gereken veriler ilgili süre boyunca saklanır ve süre sonunda silinir, yok edilir veya anonim hâle getirilir.

### 2. Personal data processed
When you use the app without an account, your data stays on your device and is not processed by us. With an account, the following is processed:
- Identity: name and surname.
- Contact: e-mail address.
- User transaction data: trip details (name, country, city, dates, budget, description), the day plan, saved places and their locations, expense records (amount, currency, category, description, payer), participant names, to-dos, packing templates, your answers and notes to the AI planner.
- Visual and document data: photos and documents you attach to trips (tickets, reservations, visas …), the address of your profile photo if you sign in with Google.
- Transaction security: account id, session data, IP address and server logs, an irreversible hash derived from the device id and a daily usage count for the AI quota.

The app does not ask for special categories of personal data. Documents you attach (e.g. passport, visa) may contain such data; they are stored only on your instruction and are not processed for any other purpose. We recommend not attaching such documents unless necessary.

### 3. Purposes
- Running the membership, authenticating you and managing your account.
- Providing the trip planning service: storing your data, syncing it between devices and sharing it with the people you choose.
- Preparing a trip plan with AI when you ask for one.
- Information security, preventing abuse and unauthorised access.
- Answering requests and complaints.
- Meeting legal obligations and requests of competent authorities.

### 4. Legal grounds and method of collection
Your personal data is collected electronically through the forms in the app and automatically while you use it, and processed on the following grounds of KVKK article 5(2):
- (c) It is necessary for concluding and performing the service contract (account, sync, sharing, planning with AI).
- (ç) It is necessary for the data controller to meet a legal obligation.
- (e) It is necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right.
- (f) It is necessary for the data controller's legitimate interests, provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed (service security, preventing abuse and quota misuse).

### 5. Recipients and transfers abroad
Your personal data may be shared, only for the purposes above, with:
- Supabase Inc. (hosting, authentication and file storage; data kept on servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
- OpenAI (USA) – only when you use planning with AI: the destination, dates, your preferences, your note and the names of saved places. Your name, e-mail, expenses and documents are not transferred.
- Google – only if you choose to sign in with Google, for the sign-in.
- Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) – for the security check that stops automated (bot) sign-ups when you sign up, sign in or reset your password with e-mail: IP address and browser information.
- Members of a trip you share – only through an invite you create: the trip's content and your name and profile photo. Your e-mail is only visible to the owner of a trip you join.
- Competent public authorities – where the law requires.

As some of these providers are located abroad, your personal data is transferred abroad. Transfers are made in line with KVKK article 9: on the basis of appropriate safeguards in the agreements with the providers, or, where that is not possible, on the basis of the explicit consent you give when using the feature concerned. Planning with AI and signing in with Google are entirely optional; you can use the app's other features without them.

### 6. Retention
Your personal data is kept while your account exists. Deleted records are permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest. When you delete your account, the trips you own (unless you hand them over to another member), their documents and your profile are deleted immediately; document and photo files are removed from the cloud immediately, or within 2 days at the latest if there is a technical problem. The cloud files of deleted documents are deleted within 31 days at the latest. Data that must be kept by law is kept for that period and then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

### 7. Your rights under KVKK article 11
By applying to the data controller you can:
- (a) learn whether your personal data is processed,
- (b) request information if it has been processed,
- (c) learn the purpose of the processing and whether it is used accordingly,
- (ç) know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
- (d) request correction if it is incomplete or incorrect,
- (e) request deletion or destruction under the conditions of KVKK article 7,
- (f) request that third parties to whom it was transferred are notified of (d) and (e),
- (g) object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis,
- (ğ) claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

### 8. How to apply
Send your request with information that lets us verify your identity by e-mail from your account's address to emrullahakkus@gmail.com, or in writing to Erciş / Van, Türkiye. Requests are answered free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest; if the request involves an additional cost, the fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board may apply.  
You can also do most corrections, copies and deletions yourself in the app: Profile → Name, Backup → Export, Trash → Delete permanently and Delete my account.
