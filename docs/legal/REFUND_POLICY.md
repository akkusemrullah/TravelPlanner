## English

Travel Planner is **free**. There are **no** ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid content; every feature, AI planning included, is free (within daily limits).

- **Subscriptions:** None. There is no auto-renewing payment or membership to cancel.
- **Refunds:** No payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund. If you see a charge for Travel Planner, it didn't come from us; contact the store's support (Google Play / App Store) and let us know at emrullahakkus@gmail.com.
- **Closing your account:** You can close your account at any time in *Profile → Delete my account*, free of charge. What happens is described on the [account and data deletion](../ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) page.
- **Future paid features:** If a paid feature is ever added, its price, renewal terms, your right of withdrawal and the refund process will be shown clearly before purchase, and this policy will be updated.
