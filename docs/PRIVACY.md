### Paylaşılan seyahatler
Bir seyahati davet koduyla paylaştığında, o seyahate katılan kişiler seyahatin içindeki her şeyi (plan, yerler, harcamalar, görevler, belgeler) görür; düzenleyiciler değişiklik de yapabilir. Seyahat üyeleri birbirinin adını ve profil fotoğrafını görür; e-posta adresleri yalnızca seyahatin sahibine görünür. Davet kodları varsayılan olarak tek kullanımlıktır ve 48 saat geçerlidir; kodu yalnızca davet ettiğin kişiyle paylaş.

### Yapay zekâ ile planlama
Bu özellik isteğe bağlıdır ve giriş yapmanı gerektirir. "Planı oluştur"a dokunduğunda şu bilgiler sunucumuzdaki plan fonksiyonuna, oradan da yapay zekâ hizmet sağlayıcısı OpenAI'ye (ABD) gönderilir: gidilecek şehir ve ülke, başlangıç tarihi ve gün sayısı, seçtiğin ilgi alanları, tempo, bütçe düzeyi, kiminle gittiğin, varsa yazdığın özel istek notu ve seyahatte zaten kayıtlı yerlerin adları. Harcamaların, belgelerin, e-posta adresin veya adın gönderilmez. Önerilen yerler, OpenStreetMap verisi kullanan haritalama hizmetleri (Photon, Overpass) üzerinden sunucumuzda doğrulanır. Plan arka planda hazırlanır; hazır olan plan uygulamana ulaşana kadar sunucumuzda tutulur, ulaşınca ve en geç 1 gün içinde silinir.  
Günlük plan hakkını uygulamak için hesap kimliğin ve cihazından türetilen, geri çevrilemeyen bir özet (SHA-256) ile günlük kullanım sayısı tutulur; cihazının asıl kimliği cihazından çıkmaz. Hesabınla giriş yaptığında ve uygulamayı açtığında da bu özet gönderilir: kötüye kullanımı (ör. plan hakkı için aynı telefonda çok sayıda hesap açılmasını) önlemek için hangi hesapların aynı cihazda kullanıldığı, hesabın e-postası ve ilk/son görülme zamanıyla birlikte, cihaz son görüldükten sonra 1 yıl tutulur ve yalnızca yönetici tarafından görülür.  
Özel istek notuna kişisel veya hassas bilgi yazmamanı öneririz. Yapay zekâ önerileri hatalı olabilir; açılış saatlerini, fiyatları ve rezervasyonları kontrol et. Uygunsuz bir öneriyi plan ekranındaki "Bildir" bağlantısıyla bize iletebilirsin.

## English

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use the Travel Planner app, where it is stored, who it is shared with and what your rights are. Users in Turkey should also see the KVKK Notice.

### In short
- You can use the app without an account (as a guest); then all your data stays only on your device.
- If you create an account, your trip data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) so it can be synced between your devices and shared.
- There are no ads, no analytics tools and no tracking. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
- Planning with AI is optional; when you use it, only what the plan needs is sent to the AI service.
- You can delete your account and your data from within the app at any time.

### Data controller and contact

E-mail: emrullahakkus@gmail.com

### Using the app as a guest
Without an account, your trips, plans, expenses, to-dos, saved places, participant names and the documents/photos you add are stored only in the app's storage on this device. We cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device.

### Data processed when you create an account
- Account data: your e-mail address, your name (or the name from Google), the address of your profile photo if you sign in with Google, and your account id. Your password is kept only as a hash by our sign-in service; we can't see it.
- Trip data: trip name, country and city, dates, budget and currency, description; the day plan and activities; saved places and their locations; expenses (amount, currency, category, date, description, who paid); participant names and cost splits; to-dos; packing templates.
- Documents and photos: tickets, reservations, visa PDFs or photos you attach to a trip. These files may contain sensitive information such as passport or ID details; we recommend attaching only the documents you really need.
- Sharing data: trip memberships, your role (owner, editor, viewer), invite codes you create, and who added or changed a record and when.
- Technical data: session data, and the IP address and request details recorded in server logs while syncing (short-lived security and troubleshooting logs).

### What we use the data for
- Creating your account, signing you in and verifying your e-mail address.
- Syncing your trip data between your devices, backing it up and sharing it with the people you choose.
- Preparing a trip plan with AI when you ask for one.
- Keeping the service secure, preventing abuse (e.g. exceeding the daily AI quota) and blocking suspended accounts.
- Answering support requests and meeting legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing.

### Shared trips
When you share a trip with an invite code, the people who join see everything in it (plan, places, expenses, to-dos, documents); editors can also change it. Trip members can see each other's name and profile photo; e-mail addresses are only visible to the trip's owner. Invite codes are single-use and valid for 48 hours by default; share a code only with the person you invite.

### Planning with AI
This feature is optional and requires an account. When you tap "Create plan", the following is sent to the plan function on our server and from there to the AI provider OpenAI (USA): the destination city and country, the start date and number of days, the interests, pace, budget level and travel companions you chose, the note you wrote (if any) and the names of places already saved in the trip. Your expenses, documents, e-mail address and name are not sent. Suggested places are checked on our server with mapping services that use OpenStreetMap data (Photon, Overpass). The plan is made in the background; a ready plan is kept on our server until it reaches your app, and deleted then, at the latest after 1 day.  
To apply the daily plan quota we keep your account id and an irreversible hash (SHA-256) derived from your device's id, with a daily usage count; your device's actual id never leaves the device. The hash is also sent when you sign in and open the app: to prevent abuse (e.g. many accounts opened on one phone for more plans), which accounts were used on the same device, with the account's e-mail and first/last seen time, is kept for 1 year after the device was last seen and is only visible to the administrator.  
Please don't put personal or sensitive information in the note. AI suggestions can be wrong; check opening hours, prices and bookings. You can report an inappropriate suggestion with the "Report" link on the plan screen.

### External services
For some features the app sends requests directly from your device to these free services:
- Open-Meteo: weather, climate averages and city search (city name, coordinates).
- Photon (komoot, OpenStreetMap data): place search and the name of a point picked on the map (search text, coordinates).
- OpenFreeMap: map images (the area shown).
- Frankfurter (European Central Bank rates): exchange rates (currency pair, date).

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. Your name, e-mail, expenses or documents are not included.  
Accounts and cloud storage are provided by Supabase (data on servers in Frankfurt, Germany). If you sign in with Google, the sign-in is handled by Google and Google's privacy policy applies. When you sign up, sign in or reset your password with e-mail, a Cloudflare Turnstile security check is used to stop automated (bot) sign-ups; during the check Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) processes your device's IP address and browser information (e.g. browser type and language). It isn't used for Google sign-in.

### Retention and deletion
- We keep your data while your account exists.
- A deleted record stays in the trash for 30 days and can be restored. When that time is up, or when you delete it permanently from the trash, it is removed from the device; the server's deleted copy is purged automatically within 30 days at the latest. The cloud files of permanently deleted documents are removed as well, right away when you are online, otherwise by the server's nightly clean-up; a document's file is removed from the cloud completely within 31 days of deleting the document at the latest.
- When you delete your account with Profile → Delete my account, the trips you own with everything in them and their files, your profile, memberships and sign-in data are deleted immediately. For each trip you share with others you first choose to hand it over to a member or to delete it for everyone; trips you hand over stay with their new owner. The document and photo files uploaded to those trips are removed from the cloud right away as well, or within 2 days at the latest if there is a connection problem. Content you added to other people's trips stays part of those trips but is no longer linked to your account.
- When you sign out you choose to keep your account's data on this device (it opens quickly next time) or to delete it from this device; your data in the cloud is not affected. The app's on-device data is not included in Android's Google backup or in device-to-device transfers.
- If you can't use the app, you can ask for your account to be deleted by writing to emrullahakkus@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account; we complete the request within 30 days.
- Server security logs are kept for a short time and then deleted.
- Action log: admin actions on your account (e.g. a ban or sign-out), the account deletion record (your account's e-mail and ID, the date, who deleted it, device/browser details and IP address) and a summary of your AI plan requests (time, outcome, duration, model used; not the plan or what you typed) are kept for 1 year for security and accountability, then deleted automatically. This record stays for 1 year after you delete your account.

### Security
All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Every table on the server is protected by row-level access rules: only a trip's members can see it and only authorised members can change it. Documents are kept in a private storage bucket. No system is perfect; if we learn of a security breach we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

### Permissions
- Notifications: trip reminders and budget alerts are scheduled on your device.
- Camera and photos: used only when you choose to add a document or photo.
- Internet: for syncing, weather, maps and exchange rates.

The app does not ask for your location and does not access your contacts.

### Backups and sharing
Backup (.zip), PDF and CSV files are created only when you ask and are sent only where you choose (e.g. Files, Drive, e-mail). Where you keep these files and who you share them with is up to you.

### Children

You can do most of this yourself in the app: change your name in Profile, take a copy with Backup → Export, and delete records and your account. For anything else, write to emrullahakkus@gmail.com. Your rights under Turkish law are described in detail in the KVKK Notice.
